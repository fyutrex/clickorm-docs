# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 25, 2024

## Overview

ClickORM is an open-source TypeScript ORM library for ClickHouse. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our documentation website and library.

## Information We Collect

### Documentation Website

When you visit our documentation website, we may collect:

- **Anonymous Analytics**: We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use our documentation. This data is anonymized and aggregated.
- **Technical Information**: Browser type, operating system, and device information to improve compatibility.
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns to improve our documentation.

### Library Usage

The ClickORM library itself:

- **Does NOT collect any data** from your application
- **Does NOT send telemetry** or usage statistics
- **Does NOT track** your database queries or operations
- Operates entirely within your infrastructure

## Third-Party Services

Our documentation may use:

- **GitHub**: For hosting our repository and handling issues
- **npm/pnpm**: For package distribution
- **Vercel/Netlify** (if applicable): For hosting documentation

These services have their own privacy policies.

## Cookies

Our documentation website may use:

- **Essential Cookies**: For basic website functionality
- **Preference Cookies**: To remember your theme preference (dark/light mode)

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Data Storage

- We do not store personal information
- Theme preferences are stored locally in your browser
- No data is transmitted to external servers from the library itself

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access any information we might have
- Request deletion of any stored data
- Opt-out of analytics (use browser extensions or disable JavaScript)

## Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
